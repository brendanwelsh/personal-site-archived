Ulanzi dial & pixel hardware

  • ulanzi-camera-switcher

    Drive a live security-camera viewer from an Ulanzi dial — works with any RTSP/ONVIF source (UniFi Protect, Reolink, Frigate).

  • ulanzi-window-control

    Drive Windows from an Ulanzi dial — rotate to scroll windows, press to focus, plus komorebi tiling controls. An Ulanzi Studio plugin.

  • ulanzi-d100h-homebrew

    Community reverse-engineering notes for the Ulanzi D100H Dial Creative Controller — HID protocol, Ulanzi Studio internals, and plugin SDK gotchas.

  • ulanzi-pixel-clock-awtrix

    Guide and curated resources for the Ulanzi TC001 Pixel Clock and AWTRIX firmware — flashing, MQTT/HTTP API, and Home Assistant.
